Before Japan

In a few days, I’ll be moving to Japan to begin a new chapter of my life.

I don’t really know what the next two years will look like yet. There will probably be a lot to learn — not only in university, but also in daily life, language, independence, and figuring things out on my own.

I made this blog because I want somewhere simple to keep the things I might otherwise forget.

No big goal. Just keep writing when something feels worth remembering.

Maybe one day I’ll come back to this first entry and laugh at how different everything turned out.
